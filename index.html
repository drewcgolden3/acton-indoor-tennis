About the club

A club that answers to its members.

The Acton Indoor Tennis Association is one of a small number of non-profit tennis clubs in the United States, dedicated to the promotion of life-long tennis. There is no owner to pay, so what members put in goes back into the courts and the programs.

Whatever your level of experience, there is a plan here for you, from competitive players to people picking up a racquet for the first time and everyone in between.
